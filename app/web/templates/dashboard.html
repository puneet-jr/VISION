Suggested 2-minute walkthrough
  1. Press Quick demo. Left: a typical detector. Right: the same detections with IDs, zones, behaviour and score.
  2. Watch the pipeline strip and the gauge react as people enter the shaded zone.
  3. In 2 Understand, click an object: five weighted factor bars and the behaviours behind its score.
  4. Press Edit zone and draw your own restricted area on the live video — scoring updates instantly.
  5. Switch Source to Phone / browser camera, scan the QR code, and run it live on a phone.
  6. When you stop, open 3 History & recordings to replay or download the annotated video.

Detection uses a general pretrained model (no accuracy claims). Scores come from transparent, configurable rules — engineering defaults, not validated threat probabilities.

Local prototype — no authentication; secure it before exposing it to a network. Tracking IDs identify objects within a session only. No face or identity recognition.
